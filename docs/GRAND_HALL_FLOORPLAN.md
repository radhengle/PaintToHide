# Grand Hall Complex — Connected World Plan

## World topology
One persistent Unity scene/world. A large central atrium is the navigation hub. Wings connect through visibly open archways and large doors. Hallways include cross-links so Seekers can loop around rather than backtrack through a single choke point.

### Ground floor
- Central: Grand Hall.
- East wing: Kitchen → Dining Room → Living Room; Living Room has a secondary opening back to the Grand Hall.
- North wing: Royal Palace → Art Gallery → Library; Library has a cross-corridor back to the atrium balcony.
- West wing: Office → Classroom → Laboratory → Workshop → Storage Room.
- South wing: Garden/Courtyard through wide glazed doors; Arcade/Game Room and Cinema connect from the south gallery corridor.
- Service corridor: Kitchen → Laundry → Storage → Garage.

### Upper floor / mezzanine
- Continuous balcony overlooking the Grand Hall with railings and multiple access points.
- Bedroom and Bathroom suite.
- Music Studio and Gym.
- Kids' Room.
- Attic reached by a stair/ladder access.
- Rooftop Terrace reached from a guarded stair door.

### Lower floor
- Basement and utility rooms reached by proper stairs from the service corridor.
- Garage/service entry connects to the exterior courtyard.

## Spatial and gameplay constraints
1. Main door clear height approximately 2.4m; standard room ceiling 4.5–5m; grand atrium substantially taller.
2. Main corridors at least 3m wide; major archways at least 3.2m clear.
3. Standard humanoid character is 1.75m tall. Door, furniture and prop scale must be relative to this baseline.
4. Door openings must have clear capsule paths and floor continuity.
5. Every floor transition must be a real staircase or validated elevator, not a teleport.
6. Garden and rooftop physically connect to the building.
7. Navigation mesh connects all accessible areas; no disconnected nav islands unless an explicit locked/secret passage is designed.
8. This is the initial authoring plan; production level art is checked against reference images before acceptance.
