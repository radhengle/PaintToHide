## Cross-room requirements
- Each room has a clear entrance from a corridor or neighbouring room.
- Openings are wide enough for the player capsule and two-way traffic.
- No room is a separate scene or disconnected teleport destination.
- Props are individually authored with visual mesh, collision proxy and object-reference metadata.
- Hiding slots are authored adjacent to props, never inside them.
- Office visual variants are reference material for one coherent Office room, not separate locations.
